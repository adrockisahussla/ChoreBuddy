ChoreBuddy Agent

Installs as a Windows service that lets you block games and browsers on your kid's PC from the ChoreBuddy app on your phone.

⬇ Download for Windows

~30 MB · Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit

Install

  1. Unzip the download anywhere (Desktop is fine).
  2. Right-click setup.cmd → Run as administrator.
  3. In the wizard: sign in with your parent Google account, pick which kid the PC is for, then Install.
  4. That's it. The agent runs in the background and watches for commands from your phone.

Control it

Open the ChoreBuddy app → drawer → Firewall → toggle the kid's PC on/off. When off, games + browsers you configured are killed and blocked instantly; trying to launch one shows a full-screen "Game time suspended" message.

Updates

Auto-updates in the background. The service checks for a new release once an hour and swaps itself in within ~5 seconds. No prompts, no kid-visible UI.

Need help? The wizard logs to C:\ProgramData\ChoreBuddy\service.log. Crashes go to crash.log in the same folder. Most "won't connect" issues come from Windows Firewall blocking the agent's outbound HTTPS — allow it through if your AV asks.
